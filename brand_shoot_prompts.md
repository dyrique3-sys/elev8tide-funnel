---

## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE RULES (bake into every image — protects the brand)
- **Products appear as SEALED vials only. NEVER in use** — no injecting, no syringes in skin, no pills in mouths, no before/after bodies, no "results."
- **No medical/weight-loss claims in any text on the image.** Sell the *feeling* and the *craft* (precision, purity, luxury, calm), never an outcome.
- Vial labels read **"RESEARCH USE ONLY"** — use the research-grade render as the product reference, **not** the real photo that says "For Subcutaneous Use."
- Lifestyle = **environments, hands, silhouettes, light** — never a person consuming the product.

---

## 🎨 THE LOOK (art direction)
- **Mood:** premium research-luxury — "a Tom Ford fragrance lab." Clinical precision + quiet wealth. Discreet, trustworthy, elevated.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#05090C` / `#0B1E22` · teal `#0F6B73`–`#128C8E` · antique gold `#C8A06A`–`#E4C98F` · warm cream `#F4EFE6`.
- **Light:** cinematic low-key, a single warm gold rim light, soft volumetric haze, deep shadows, glossy wet reflections.
- **Textures:** amber glass vials, brushed gold caps, wet black stone, water droplets, eucalyptus sprigs, gold dust / bokeh, frosted glass, marble.
- **Lens:** 85mm macro, shallow depth of field, studio product photography.

### ⭐ STYLE SUFFIX — append to EVERY image prompt:
> *"— 85mm macro, cinematic low-key studio lighting, single warm gold rim light, deep navy-and-teal background, soft volumetric haze, glossy reflections, antique gold accents, ultra-detailed, photorealistic, premium luxury product photography, 8k"*

## RUN ORDER (how to shoot the whole brand efficiently)
1. **Lock the look:** generate A1 first. Approve the vial + lighting. That becomes your reference for everything.
2. **Product set:** A1–A5 (your workhorses — reused across site, shop, emails).
3. **Backgrounds:** B1–B3 + C1–C2 (banners + heroes).
4. **Placements:** D (emails), E (social), F (stories), G (flyers).
5. **Motion:** H1–H3 last (most credits).
> Reuse aggressively — one great A1 hero covers the homepage, a shop card, and an email. You don't need a unique image for every slot.
