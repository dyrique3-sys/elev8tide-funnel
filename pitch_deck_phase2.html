Straight talk on payments

The one piece that depends on approval

Live checkout and subscriptions need a payment processor that will approve your specific product category. That approval isn't something a developer can force — it's the processor's call, and mainstream ones (Stripe, Square, PayPal) typically won't touch this category.

So here's the honest plan: in Phase 2 I build everything ready for checkout and map out which high-risk providers can approve you. Turning live payments on happens as a focused step once you're approved with a compliant processor — kept separate so nothing's built that stays blocked outside our control.

Per your own agenda: we confirm product regulations + the provider's policies before activating any checkout or product promotion.
