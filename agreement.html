1Scope of Services

Provider will design, build and configure a marketing & automation system (“Phase 2 — Sales Engine”) consisting of:

  • Email Sales Engine — a behavior-branched nurture sequence plus ongoing, seasonal campaigns.
  • SMS Marketing — behavior-triggered texts and seasonal campaigns with tracking.
  • Social + AI Automation — comment-to-DM funnels and AI-assisted reply automation.
  • Lead HQ, Dashboard & Secure Backup — CRM organization, source tracking, and an off-platform backup of Client’s contact data.
  • Frictionless Payment System — an order-number checkout flow paid via Client’s chosen peer-to-peer methods (e.g., Cash App, Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay).
